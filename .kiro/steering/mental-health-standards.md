# University Student Mental Health & Crisis Guidelines

## Institutional Context (Indian Higher Education)
- Tailored for students coping with competitive entrance examinations, mid-semester pressure, hostel isolation, homesickness, and career anxieties.
- Follows University Grants Commission (UGC) Guidelines on Promotion of Physical and Mental Well-being in Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs).
- Operates under the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 respecting individual student dignity and autonomy.

## Crisis Triage Protocols
- Immediate distress keywords ("suicide", "end my life", "harm myself", "want to die") must trigger an inescapable Crisis Banner with direct one-tap dialing:
  - Tele-MANAS: 14416 / 1800-891-4416 (Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, 24/7, 20+ Indian languages)
  - KIRAN Mental Health Helpline: 1800-599-0019 (DEPwD, 24/7)
  - Emergency Services: 112
- Non-crisis screenings (PHQ-4) must explicitly state they are educational self-assessments, not clinical diagnostic verdicts.
