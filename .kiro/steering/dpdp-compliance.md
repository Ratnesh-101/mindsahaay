# India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Steering

## Core Principles
1. Consent & Purpose Limitation: Data is collected solely for student mental well-being support and is never shared with university administration, professors, or third parties.
2. Local-First / Client-Side Encryption: Journal entries and self-care notes are stored on-device with optional zero-knowledge sync.
3. Right to Information & Access: Students can download a machine-readable JSON copy of all logged moods, appointments, and journal entries with a single click.
4. Right to Erasure / Forgotten: Students can wipe all local and remote data instantly without requiring administrative approval.
